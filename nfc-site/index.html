FAQ

Good questions. Straight answers.

What does Pixel Studios do?

Four studios work together: Design (logos, brand identity, social media, flyer design and digital menus); Print (cards, flyers, stickers, apparel, packaging and more); Smart Cards (Direct and Premium Portfolio Cards); and Mobile & Web Development (websites, e-commerce, apps and business systems).

What is the difference between the two Smart Cards?

A Direct Smart Card opens one destination you choose, such as WhatsApp, Instagram or a website. A Premium Portfolio Card opens your own editable business page with profile information, services, gallery and contact or order actions.

Do Smart Cards need an app?

No. Visitors tap an NFC-enabled phone or scan the printed QR code; the destination opens in the phone's browser.

How much do design and print products cost?

Current examples: flyer design from ₦10,000, logo design from ₦25,000, brand identity from ₦60,000, custom mugs from ₦5,500, T-shirts from ₦8,000 and business cards from ₦15,000 per 100. See the Pricing and Services sections for more catalogue prices.

How much do websites and apps cost?

The app catalogue currently lists business websites from ₦150,000, e-commerce stores from ₦250,000 and mobile apps from ₦400,000. Business systems are quoted to scope. Share the features you need for an exact quote.

Can you create a digital menu for my restaurant?

Yes. The Design studio offers a digital menu page with QR options, built to be updated as your menu changes. Tell us if you also need printed menus or table cards.

Can I request a custom design or send a reference?

Yes. Share your reference, text, sizes, colours, quantity, budget and deadline in your brief. The team will confirm the scope and quote before production begins.

How long will my order or project take?

Timing depends on the product and scope. We confirm the schedule before work starts; print orders and digital projects have different production and review milestones.

Do you work outside Gusau?

Pixel Studios is based in Gusau, Zamfara State. Remote design and digital projects can be discussed, and print delivery options should be confirmed with the team for your destination.
