FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do the people I share with need an app?

Never. A tap (or QR scan) opens your page directly in their browser — iPhone, Android, any phone. That's the whole point.

What if my details change after printing?

Your card points to your live page, not to static info. Change your number, prices, photos or links anytime from your dashboard — the card keeps working exactly as it is.

Which phones support the NFC tap?

iPhone XS and newer tap with no setup. Most Android phones from the last 6 years have NFC built in. For every other phone, the printed QR code on the card opens the same page.

How long does delivery take?

Design, printing and programming take about 48 hours. Delivery nationwide after that — or pick up at our studio in Gusau.

What happens if I lose my card?

Your page and its data are safe in your account. Pause the lost device from the dashboard and order a replacement programmed to the same page.
